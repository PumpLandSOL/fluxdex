// the percolator engine

Solvency isn't a promise. It's the math.

FluxDEX runs a port of percolator, the risk engine from toly's open-source work. Three invariants keep the exchange whole.

01 / HAIRCUT

Capital is senior.

Your collateral always comes back first. Profit is paid at a haircut H, so nobody can ever withdraw more value than sits on the balance sheet.

H = min(Residual, ΣPnL⁺) / ΣPnL⁺
02 / A·K·F INDICES

No queues. No ADL lottery.

Funding, marks and deleveraging settle through lazy per-side indices: every account updates in O(1), with nobody skipping the line.

K += A·ΔP  ·  F ±= A·funding
03 / BOUNDED CRANKS

No single-tick blowups.

Each price update can only move the mark so far per slot. A bad tick or oracle spike can't liquidate the book in one shot.

|ΔP|·1e4 ≤ max_move · Δt · P
